## Corrections the final report must make

1. EACL Table 3: say "Aya Collection" (a benchmark), model is Llama-PLLuM-8B-instruct (Polish-adapted); Belebele PL 0.609 vs EN 0.656 (EN higher), Aya PL 0.656 vs EN 0.552 (PL higher). Mapping verified. Do not call it like-for-like: Belebele is parallel by design but the paper does not state paired sampling (900 stratified per dataset); Aya PL/EN are not parallel; all LLM-judge scored.
2. LREC: report 81.5% only with the order caveat (54.3% when order reversed; Qwen judge favours PLLuM when reversed). Confirm no English baseline.
3. CUS-QA: label columns cs/sk/uk/en per block; 'en' = annotators' human English translation of the same Q&A, others machine-translated; authors' conclusion is mixed ("sometimes" better in English), present in abstract and Sec 5.3 (WS5 said it was unconfirmed - wrong). Direction flips between LLM-judge (Tables 11, 15) and human eval (Table 10).
4. OneRuler: add cs = Svejk vol.3, ru = War and Peace, sr = Dragutin Ilic, uk = Marko Vovchok; no Slovak/Slovene; model list: add Gemini 1.5 Flash, DeepSeek-R1 English-only.
5. Karpinska: significance statement is journalist's paraphrase attributed to her in a PAP article, not a quote and not in the paper; press only.
6. Leaderboard: mark scores UNREACHABLE (401).
7. Flag Tokka-Bench date anomaly; fertility numbers in 2605.24718 remain unverified by this check.

## NEW findings
- MMLU-ProX Table 3 (above): the only genuinely same-question, same-model Slavic-vs-English table I read; English wins slightly in every cell; Polish absent.
