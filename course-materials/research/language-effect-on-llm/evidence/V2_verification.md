## NEW like-for-like finding (most valuable): mE5-large, English vs Polish, same model, same-origin datasets

Sources: English = multilingual-E5 report, arXiv 2402.05672, Table 7 (MTEB per-dataset, mE5-large; metric not labelled in the table caption, MTEB retrieval default is nDCG@10). Polish = PL-MTEB, arXiv 2405.10138 (v2), Table 7 (nDCG@10). PL datasets are machine-translated BEIR (BEIR-PL). Only the six datasets that exist in both without "HardNeg" sub-sampling are used (HardNeg variants for DBPedia, HotpotQA, MSMARCO, NQ, Quora use reduced corpora and are not comparable).

| Dataset | EN | PL | Diff |
|---|---|---|---|
| ArguAna | 54.4 | 52.99 | -1.41 |
| FiQA | 43.8 | 32.97 | -10.83 |
| NFCorpus | 34.0 | 30.21 | -3.79 |
| SCIDOCS | 17.5 | 13.82 | -3.68 |
| SciFact | 70.4 | 65.66 | -4.74 |
| TREC-COVID | 71.3 | 69.86 | -1.44 |
| Mean (recomputed) | 48.57 | 44.25 | -4.32 |

For reference, BM25 on the same six datasets (BEIR-PL Table 3): EN 43.57 vs PL 38.31 = -5.26 (ArguAna 47.2/41.36, FiQA 25.4/19.0, NFCorpus 34.3/31.9, SciDocs 16.5/14.1, SciFact 69.1/62.5, TREC-COVID 68.9/61.0).

Interpretation: on translated-BEIR data with the same model, Polish is about 4.3 nDCG@10 points lower than English for mE5-large (6 datasets, range -1.4 to -10.8), slightly smaller than the BM25 gap (-5.3). Caveats: (1) Polish corpora are Google-Translate outputs, so translation noise and language are confounded; (2) results come from two different papers/evaluation runs (possible differences in prefixes "query:"/"passage:", truncation, corpus versions); (3) only 6 datasets, one model, 2023-24 generation; (4) PL-MTEB table checks out internally (mE5-large mean of 11 recomputed = 52.43). Not obtained for Qwen3/bge-m3.

PL-MTEB paper itself reports no English results; bge-m3 paper MKQA has no English row.
